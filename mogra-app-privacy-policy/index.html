    mogra — privacy policy

    Last updated: 6 September 2026. Applies to the Android app mogra (com.mogralabs.mogra).

    The short version

    mogra collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way to. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. Everything the app does happens on your phone.

    What the app does with your microphone

    mogra listens in two places: when you hum your Sa, and when you record a passage for the Raag Identifier. In both cases the recording is held in the phone’s memory for as long as you are on that screen, analysed there, and discarded. It is never written to storage and never leaves the device.

    Android asks for microphone permission the first time you record, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Apps → mogra → Permissions. The app records only while you have pressed record; there is no background listening.

    The app cannot send anything anywhere

    Microphone access is the only permission mogra requests. In particular it does not request internet access, so the app is not merely unwilling to transmit your recordings — it is technically unable to. Anyone can check this: the permissions an Android app holds are listed on its Play Store page and in Settings.

    The model that identifies raags runs on the phone. Nothing is uploaded for analysis.

    What is stored on your device

    Two small settings, and nothing else:

    • the Sa you last chose, as a number in Hz;
    • the language you last chose.

    Both are ordinary app settings, readable only by mogra, and both are deleted when you uninstall the app. The app also unpacks its own model files into its private storage the first time you run an identification; these are part of the app, not anything about you.

    No analytics, no advertising, no third parties

    mogra contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party software-development kits that gather information. Nobody — including us — receives usage data, device identifiers, or any record of what you sang.

    Children

    mogra is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

    Raag data

    The raag information the app shows — aaroha, avaroha, mukhyanga, nyas swars, vaadi, samvaadi, thaat and prahar — is drawn from tanarang.com and is bundled with the app. Looking a raag up involves no network request.

    Changes to this policy

    If the app ever gains a feature that changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
